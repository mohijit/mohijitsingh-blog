Diagrams compile from TikZ at build time, so there's no client-side cost:

```tikz
\draw[thick,->] (0,0) -- (3,0) node[right] {$x$};
\draw[thick,->] (0,0) -- (0,3) node[above] {$y$};
\draw[blue,thick] (0,0) circle (2);
\node at (1.4,1.4) {$x^2+y^2=r^2$};
```

And charts are interactive (hover for values) — the sigmoid function:

<Chart fn="1 / (1 + Math.exp(-x))" domain={[-8, 8]} xLabel="x" yLabel="σ(x)" />
